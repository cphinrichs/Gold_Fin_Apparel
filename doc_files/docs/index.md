# Gold Fin Apparel Overview

Gold Fin Apparel is a customizable clothing product line focused on premium shirts that customers can tailor through a variety of selectable attributes. Customers can personalize shirts based on size, style, design, material and color.

Cost is calculated consistently to ensure clear and reliable pricing while enabling flexible product-mix combinations.
