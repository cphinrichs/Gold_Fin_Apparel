# Gold Fin Apparel User Guide

Welcome to Gold Fin Apparel. This guide provides step-by-step instructions for browsing products, customizing your selections, managing your cart, and completing your purchase.

## Navigating the site

1. Open your web browser and navigate to the Gold Fin Apparel website at [www.goldfinapparel.com](http://www.goldfinapparel.com).

1. Use the navigation bar at the top of the page to access different sections of the site, including **Home**, **Browse**, **Cart**, **Help**, and **About**.

    - **Home**: Return to the featured products carousel
    - **Browse**: View all available apparel styles
    - **Cart**: Review selected items and proceed to checkout
    - **Help**: Access size charts, shipping information, and return policies
    - **About**: Learn more about Gold Fin Apparel

1. Click **Browse** to start exploring product offerings.

    Browse products by category, price, size, and other filters to find the perfect apparel for you.

1. Click on any product image or name to view detailed information and customization options.

    You can select from various designs, sizes, colors, and materials to create a personalized item. The product page also displays multiple images, price information, and a detailed description to help you make an informed decision. View **Customizing your selection** for more details on how to customize your apparel.

1. Click **Add to Cart** when you have finalized your selection. You can review and modify your cart before proceeding to checkout.

1. When you are ready to complete your purchase, click **Cart** in the navigation bar to review your selected items and proceed to checkout. View **Managing your cart** for instructions on how to review and modify your cart contents.

1. Follow the checkout instructions to enter your shipping and payment information, review your order, and complete your purchase. View **Completing your purchase** for detailed checkout instructions.

1. After completing your purchase, you will receive an order confirmation email with details about your order and estimated delivery date.

## Customizing your selection

### Selecting a design

- On the product inspection page, review available designs.
- Click on the **Featured Designs** at the top of the page or browse additional designs in the design selector.
- Each design is displayed with a preview image.

### Choosing size

- Click the **Size** button corresponding to your desired size.
- Available sizes are displayed as selectable buttons.
- Unavailable sizes are grayed out and cannot be selected.

!!! note "Size selection"

    If you are unsure about sizing, consult the **Size Charts** in the Help section. Size charts are available by product type (T-shirt, Vest, Kimono, etc.).

### Selecting color

- Click the **Color** button to view available color options.
- A color palette or list of available colors appears.
- Click on your preferred color.

### Choosing material

- Click the **Material** button to view material options.
- Available materials are displayed with texture previews where applicable.
- Click on your preferred material.

!!! tip "Material compatibility"

    Not all colors and materials are available for every design and size combination. When you select an option, incompatible choices are automatically disabled.

## Managing your cart

### Adding items to your cart

1. After customizing your product selection, specify the desired quantity.
1. Click the **Add to Cart** button.
1. The item is added to your cart and a confirmation message appears.

### Reviewing your cart

Click **Cart** in the navigation bar. Your cart displays:

- Selected items with customization details (design, size, color, material)
- Price per item and subtotal
- Total cart value
- Quantity for each item

### Modifying cart items

#### Changing quantity

1. Locate the item you want to modify in the cart.
1. Adjust the quantity field or use the quantity adjustment controls.
1. The cart total updates automatically.

#### Removing items

1. Click **Remove** next to an item you want to delete.
1. A confirmation dialog appears.
1. Confirm the removal. The item is deleted from your cart.

#### Clearing your cart

1. Click **Clear Cart** in the cart view.
1. A confirmation dialog appears asking you to confirm.
1. Click **Confirm** to remove all items from your cart.

!!! warning "Cart persistence"

    Your cart is automatically saved to your browser's local storage. If you close your browser, your cart contents are preserved and available when you return.

## Completing your purchase

### Accessing checkout

1. Review your cart to ensure all selections are correct.
1. Click the **Checkout** button.
1. The checkout page displays an order summary.

### Entering shipping information

1. In the **Shipping Information** section, enter:
    - Full name
    - Street address
    - City
    - State or province
    - Postal code
    - Country

1. Select your preferred **Delivery Method**:
   - **Standard Delivery**: Estimated delivery in 5-7 business days
   - **Express Delivery**: Estimated delivery in 2-3 business days

### Entering payment information

In the **Payment Information** section, enter:

- Cardholder name
- Card number
- Expiration date
- CVV (card security code)

### Reviewing your order

Before completing your order:

1. Verify all items in your order summary
1. Confirm shipping address and delivery method
1. Review itemized costs:
   - Subtotal
   - Estimated shipping cost
   - Applicable taxes
   - **Order total**

1. Verify estimated delivery date

### Completing the purchase

1. Click the **Complete Order** button.
1. Your order is processed and confirmed.
1. You are directed to an **Order Confirmation** page displaying:
   - Order confirmation number
   - Order details and itemized costs
   - Shipping address
   - Estimated delivery date
   - Next steps for order tracking

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot select certain options

**Issue**: Some color, size, or material options are grayed out and cannot be selected.

**Solution**: Not all combinations of design, color, material, and size are available. Try selecting a different combination.

### My cart is empty

**Issue**: Items I added to my cart are missing.

**Solution**:

- Check if you cleared your cart accidentally
- Try clearing your browser cache and cookies, then reload the page

### Payment is not processing

**Issue**: My payment is declined during checkout.

**Solution**:

- Verify your card number, expiration date, and CVV are correct
- Ensure your billing address matches your card information
- Try a different payment method
- Contact your bank to verify the card is not blocked

### I cannot complete my order

**Issue**: The Complete Order button is inactive or not responding.

**Solution**:

- Verify all required fields in the shipping and payment sections are completed
- Ensure your cart contains at least one item
- Check your internet connection
- Try reloading the page
- Use a different browser if the issue persists
